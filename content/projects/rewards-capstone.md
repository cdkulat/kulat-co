## What it does

A working prototype that lets Home Depot reward employees for improving customer service,
built from Home Depot's source data.

## How it was run

Industry partner capstone at Kennesaw State, sponsored by Home Depot. The team met regularly with
Home Depot stakeholders through discovery and build, following an agile delivery process.

## My role

Product designer and engineer. I designed the gamification system and the UI, and built the Vue.js
frontend and the Node.js backend.
